Synchronicity Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Synchronicity is a private journal. It is built so that what you write stays with you. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

The short version

  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your journal, photos, voice memos and wellness logs are stored only on your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

Everything you add to Synchronicity (journal entries, photos, voice memos, tagged locations, sleep, nutrition, movement, fasting and goal logs, reminders and settings) is saved in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions

The app asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera and Photos: to attach a photo to an entry, and to spot numbers in a photo (text recognition runs on your iPhone).
  • Microphone: to record a voice memo.
  • Speech Recognition: to turn your voice memos into text. Transcription runs entirely on your iPhone; recordings are never sent to Apple or anyone else.
  • Location: to tag where an entry was written and to show local weather and the night sky.
  • Motion & Fitness: to show daily steps, note whether you were walking, driving or still, aim the star map when you point your phone at the sky, and read air pressure from the barometer.
  • Apple Health (optional): to read your sleep (to fill in your sleep log and prompt you about dreams when you wake) and to save meditation and breathwork sessions as Mindful Minutes. Health data stays on your iPhone, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with anyone.
  • Face ID: to unlock the app if you turn on App Lock. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the app never sees your face data.

Weather

To show a forecast, the app sends your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to a weather service: Apple Weather, or Open-Meteo if Apple Weather is unavailable. Only the coordinates are sent, never your journal or any identifier. These services handle the request under their own privacy policies (Apple, Open-Meteo).

Ocean conditions

For tides, surf and water temperature, the app downloads public lists of NOAA stations and picks the nearest one on your iPhone. It then asks NOAA for that station's data. Your location is never sent.

On California coasts, beach water quality comes from the State Water Resources Control Board (data.ca.gov). To find nearby beaches, the app sends a search area of roughly 25 km around your approximate location. Your exact location is not sent.

In Washington, the app downloads the state's list of beach advisories and closures from the Department of Ecology and picks the nearest beaches on your iPhone, so no location is sent.

Exporting and sharing

You can export your data or share a composition using the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Purchases

The optional Premium unlock and its free trial are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether Premium or the trial is active on your Apple ID.

Children

Synchronicity doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
