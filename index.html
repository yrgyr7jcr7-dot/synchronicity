Synchronicity Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Synchronicity is a private journal. It is built so that what you write stays with you. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

The short version

  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your journal, photos, voice memos and wellness logs are stored only on your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

Everything you add to Synchronicity (journal entries, photos, voice memos, tagged locations, sleep, nutrition, movement, fasting and goal logs, reminders and settings) is saved in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions

The app asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera and Photos: to attach a photo to an entry.
  • Microphone: to record a voice memo.
  • Location: to tag where an entry was written and to show local weather and the night sky.
  • Motion & Fitness: to show daily steps, note whether you were walking, driving or still, and to aim the star map when you point your phone at the sky.
  • Face ID: to unlock the app if you turn on App Lock. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the app never sees your face data.

Weather

To show a forecast, the app sends your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to a weather service: Apple Weather, or Open-Meteo if Apple Weather is unavailable. Only the coordinates are sent, never your journal or any identifier. These services handle the request under their own privacy policies (Apple, Open-Meteo).

Exporting and sharing

You can export your data or share a composition using the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Children

Synchronicity doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
